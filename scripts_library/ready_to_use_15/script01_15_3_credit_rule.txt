If you pay your credit card bill on the due date, you are quietly hurting your credit score. Banks do not report your balance on your due date, they report it on your statement closing date. That means if your limit is 1,000 dollars and you spend 500, your utilization reports as 50 percent, which tanks your score. Instead, use the 15/3 rule: pay half 15 days before your statement, and the rest 3 days before. Save this reel and follow The Wealth Blueprint for more money rules.

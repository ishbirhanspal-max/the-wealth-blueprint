If you have a low credit score or zero credit history, here is the fastest legal shortcut. It is called becoming an Authorized User. If a parent or relative has a credit card with an 8-year clean payment history and a high limit, they can add you as an authorized user. Their entire 8-year perfect record instantly copies onto your credit report. You do not even need to touch the physical card. Save this reel and follow for daily finance loopholes.

THE TRAP:
THE DEFAULT TAX TRAP
The government made the New Tax Regime default. If you have a home loan or high rent and blindly choose the default, you could overpay ₹50,000 to ₹1.5 Lakh in unnecessary taxes.

THE BLUEPRINT:
THE ₹3.75 LAKH DEDUCTION RULE
Calculate your total deductions:
• Standard Deduction: ₹75,000
• 80C (PPF/EPF/ELSS): ₹1,50,000
• 80D (Health Insurance): ₹25,000 to ₹50,000

*If total deductions exceed ₹3.75 Lakhs, choose OLD REGIME!*

THE OPTIMAL TAX SAVINGS

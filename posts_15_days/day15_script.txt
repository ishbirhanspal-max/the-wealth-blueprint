Robert Kiyosaki said it best in Rich Dad Poor Dad: the rich buy assets, while the poor buy liabilities they think are assets. An asset puts money into your pocket whether you work or not: dividend index funds, cash-flowing real estate, automated software. A liability takes money out of your pocket: your car loan, your subscriptions, luxury items. Spend your twenties buying assets that eventually pay for your luxury. Welcome to The Wealth Blueprint.

If an online company refuses to refund you for a damaged product or hits you with an unauthorized recurring fee, never argue with their customer service. Under federal law, you have the legal right to file a Chargeback with your credit card issuer within 60 days. The bank immediately clawbacks the money from the merchant while they investigate. Most merchants will resolve your issue instantly rather than pay a 25 dollar chargeback fee. Share this with someone who shops online.
